bullseye ARC

vioplayer.support@gmail.com

Use this address for app support, privacy questions and requests to delete support correspondence.

For playback or app issues

Include your VIO version, Android version, phone model, what happened and the steps to reproduce it. For playback problems, mention the media format if known. Share a screenshot only if it contains no private information.

Do not send passwords, identity documents, private media or streaming links containing access tokens. Sending diagnostic information is voluntary. There is no automatic upload from this page.

For privacy or deletion

Use the subject VIO player — privacy request or VIO player — data deletion request. Describe your request and, if relevant, the earlier support conversation. We cannot remotely erase files on your phone; follow the Delete Data guide for local data.
