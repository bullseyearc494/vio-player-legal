01. Local media and app preferences

With your permission, VIO reads video, audio and subtitle files and metadata such as names, locations, sizes, dates and durations. It reads or generates thumbnails and artwork to display your library and play the media you choose.

Library information, watched positions, last-played items, favourites, playlists, bookmarks, saved queues, track selections, theme choices and other preferences are kept on your device where the corresponding feature is used or enabled. Stream addresses can be retained in saved playback or queue information. Playback and export may create temporary files.

We do not provide a VIO account or cloud backup/synchronisation of your media, Vault or playback history. Local media is not uploaded to us merely because it appears in the library. Advertising, Analytics and billing involve separate data flows described below; the app as a whole should not be understood as “no data collected”.

File operations you choose can copy, modify, export, share, move or delete the selected media. Recycled files remain locally until restored, deleted or expired by Android. On Android 8–10, VIO keeps its recycle-bin copies in private app storage.

02. Permissions and Android features

Media or storage permissions support the device library; older Android versions may require write permission for file changes. You can use selected-file access where supported, use the system picker, or manage access in Android Settings. VIO does not require contacts, camera, microphone or precise-location permission for its media-player features.

Internet access supports ads, Analytics, applicable consent messaging, purchases and streams you open. Background playback uses a foreground media service and wake-lock capabilities. Audio settings support playback controls. Android notifications, the lock screen, picture-in-picture or connected devices can display a playing title or artwork. Lock-screen artwork can be disabled in VIO settings.

03. Google AdMob: collection and sharing

Ad-supported releases use Google AdMob. When its Mobile Ads SDK operates, it collects and shares data with Google for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention. Depending on SDK settings, device availability and privacy choices, this includes:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Device/account identifiers: advertising ID, app set ID and applicable account-related identifiers.
  • App/ad interactions: launches, taps, ad impressions and video-ad engagement.
  • Diagnostics: performance, launch timing, hangs and energy-use information.

The SDK encrypts these transmissions using TLS. This disclosure concerns advertising telemetry; it does not mean VIO uploads your videos or Vault files. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and the legacy SDK disclosure.

Google and advertising partners involved in serving an ad process information under their applicable policies and privacy choices. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Partner details relevant to consent are shown in the applicable consent message. Advertising data is not limited to information we can see directly.

We do not sell your media or Vault contents. Advertising-related transfers can fall within legal definitions of “sharing”, targeted advertising or “sale” in some jurisdictions; use the applicable privacy choices below.

04. Advertising consent and your choices

Where required, ad-enabled releases present a consent or privacy message before the processing that requires consent. This includes applicable requirements in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. You can review or withdraw relevant choices through the app’s advertising Privacy options entry when available for your region. The Google User Messaging Platform or another Google-certified consent platform manages the applicable message.

Refusing personalised advertising does not necessarily mean no ads or no data processing. Non-personalised or limited ads may still need permitted technical data for delivery, security or measurement. The options and legal basis depend on your region and the message shown. Do not treat use of the app or acceptance of its Terms as consent to personalised ads.

Where applicable US state privacy law provides an opt-out of sale/sharing or targeted advertising, use the available privacy message or contact us. You can also manage your Android advertising ID in Android’s privacy/ads settings and Google preferences through My Ad Center. These controls have different scopes; resetting an ID does not erase all previously held data.

Consent choices and related records may be stored on-device and processed by the consent service to apply your choices. Premium removes VIO-served ads while the entitlement is active; it does not erase earlier ad records or stop Analytics governed by its own choices, necessary purchase verification, support or streams you choose to open.

05. Google Analytics for Firebase

Releases with Analytics enabled use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand feature usage and improve VIO. Google processes information for the Analytics service, and we access reports in our Firebase/Google Analytics project.

Depending on consent and configuration, data includes an app-instance identifier; app launches, sessions and engagement; feature/screen events; app version, device/operating-system details and language; approximate geographic information; and supported ad or purchase events. Advertising identifiers can also be used where available and enabled. These are pseudonymous identifiers, not a promise of anonymity. See Google’s Analytics data-collection explanation.

Our Analytics events are intended to describe app functions, such as use of playback controls or theme selection. They must not contain your video/audio files, filenames, folder paths, subtitle text, private stream URLs, Vault contents or unlock secrets. We do not use your email address or name as an Analytics user identifier. Adding Analytics does not upload your media to Firebase Storage or create a VIO account.

Where required, collection waits for the applicable permission or consent. Analytics-enabled releases provide an Usage analytics control to stop future collection. Analytics consent is separate from personalised-ad consent, and buying ad-free Premium does not by itself turn Analytics off. If Analytics is linked to advertising services, advertising-related use is subject to applicable settings and consent. See Analytics controls and deletion.

We use retention controls in the linked Analytics property for user/event-level records, retaining them only as needed for usage analysis and improvement. The configured retention period and whether it resets on new activity affect deletion timing; standard aggregated reports can remain longer. Contact us about the applicable retention setting or a deletion request. Read Google’s retention explanation and Firebase privacy information.

06. Premium subscriptions and Themes

When purchasing is enabled, monthly and yearly Premium plans use Google Play Billing. Google processes payment and account information. VIO receives purchase information needed to recognise and manage your entitlement, such as the product/plan, purchase token or order identifier, purchase time and purchase/subscription status. We do not receive your full payment-card details or Google password.

Purchase information is used for entitlement checks, restoration, fraud prevention and purchase support. Theme selection is stored locally; Premium access determines which themes are unlocked. A billing query can occur when opening the app or restoring access. If you contact us about a purchase, the order reference and information you provide become part of that support correspondence.

See Premium terms for billing, renewal and cancellation, and Google’s Privacy Policy for Google’s payment/account data handling.

07. Device-only encrypted Private Vault

In releases providing Private Vault, the feature is designed to keep selected files encrypted on your phone. It does not provide cloud upload, cloud backup or cross-device synchronisation. Access-protection information and local cryptographic material are used on the device to protect the Vault. We do not need your unlock secret, and you should never send it to support.

Encryption protects stored Vault files; it does not secure an unlocked or compromised phone absolutely. Decrypted content must be available locally during authorised viewing. Exporting or sharing a file creates or releases a copy outside the Vault; any original, backup or previously shared copy may still exist. Do not assume moving a file into the Vault removes every other copy.

We do not hold a cloud copy of your Vault or offer server-side recovery of its files. Losing access credentials, local keys or app storage can make files unrecoverable. Before resetting, uninstalling or changing devices, export any files you want to keep using the available Vault controls. See Vault deletion guidance. No particular algorithm or recovery guarantee is implied by this policy.

08. Streams, sharing, support and service providers

When you open a network stream, the source and servers it redirects to receive your IP address, requested address and technical request information. Sharing media sends it to the app or recipient you choose. These services apply their own privacy practices.

Support emails provide us with your email address, message and voluntary attachments. We use them to respond, investigate issues and manage requests. Our email provider processes and stores this correspondence. Do not send private media, Vault secrets, passwords or stream URLs containing access tokens.

Google supplies advertising, Analytics, consent and purchase services when enabled; our email provider handles support delivery; GitHub hosts these pages. Information can be processed in countries outside your own under the relevant provider’s safeguards. We may disclose information we hold where required by law or necessary to address fraud or legal claims.

09. Retention, security and deletion

Local app information remains until you remove it, clear app storage, uninstall, or Android removes temporary data. Turning off history does not necessarily remove existing records. Shared-storage media and exported copies remain until separately deleted. Android-managed trash follows system expiry; the Android 8–10 VIO recycle bin has no automatic expiry implemented.

Private preferences use Android app-storage protections; VIO disables app backup for its private data. Vault encryption is an additional protection where the Vault feature is enabled. No storage or transfer method can guarantee absolute security. HTTPS streams encrypt transit, but a user-opened HTTP stream is unencrypted; avoid HTTP URLs containing private information or credentials.

Purchase-related information is retained as needed to manage access, restoration, disputes and applicable accounting/legal obligations. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve requests and maintain necessary support or legal records. Google’s ad, consent and transaction records follow Google’s retention rules, settings and applicable obligations; we cannot promise an automatic deletion date for records controlled by Google.

Use Delete Data for local removal and requests concerning information we hold. We review deletion requests and explain applicable exceptions. Clearing VIO storage does not automatically delete Google’s records, cancel a subscription or erase copies shared elsewhere.

Rights concerning data held independently by Google or another provider may need to be exercised with that provider.

11. Children and family use

Local playback does not require a name, date of birth or VIO account. This is not a curated children’s media service; the user chooses the content. Ad treatment must reflect the app’s declared audience and any applicable under-age status. Where child-directed or under-age rules apply, applicable restrictions on personalised advertising and data use must be respected.

If you believe a child sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate. Parents or guardians can use the device’s permissions and deletion controls. This paragraph does not classify the app as suitable for all ages.
