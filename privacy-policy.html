Privacy Policy

Clear about your data.

This policy explains how VIO player handles information when you play and manage media, use optional online features, or visit this website.

App: VIO playerPackage: com.vio.playerUpdated: 4 October 2026

VIO player is provided by bullseye ARC (“we”, “us”). Contact us at vioplayer.support@gmail.com for privacy questions. This policy describes the current app, version 1.4.0. Later releases that change data handling will require an updated policy.

01. Media and information on your device

With your permission, VIO reads video and audio files and their metadata, such as names, folders, file locations or content identifiers, duration, size and dates, to build your library and play media. It reads or generates thumbnails and artwork, and reads subtitle files you choose.

VIO stores library information, playback positions, last-played items, saved queues, favourites, playlists, bookmarks, track selections and app preferences locally where the relevant feature is used or enabled. Temporary files and cached information may be created during playback and media processing. Network stream addresses may be saved in local queue or playback information.

Media management actions you request, such as copy, move, rename, export, share or delete, access or modify the selected files. Recycled files may remain on the device until restored, permanently deleted or expired by Android. On Android 8–10, VIO stores its recycle-bin copies in private app storage.

We do not operate a VIO account, cloud media library or server that receives your local media, library or playback history. The current app has no advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell your personal data.

02. Permissions and system features

Media permissions let VIO display and play your device library. Older Android versions use storage permissions; older-device file changes may also require write permission. You can grant access only to selected items where Android supports it, use the system file picker, or change permissions in Android Settings.

Internet access supports streams you open. Foreground playback and wake-lock capabilities support playback while the app is in the background. Audio settings support volume and audio controls. VIO does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera or microphone.

Android media controls, the lock screen, paired devices and system-managed picture-in-picture can display the playing title and artwork. You can turn off lock-screen artwork in VIO settings. Other system visibility is controlled by Android.

03. When information leaves your device

  • Network streams: opening an internet media address connects to that provider and any servers it redirects to. They receive your IP address, requested URL and technical request information needed to deliver the stream. Their own privacy policies apply. Use sources you trust.
  • Sharing and external apps: when you choose to share a file or open a link, the selected app or service receives the information needed for that action. Its handling is outside VIO’s local storage.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the issue. Our email provider processes the correspondence to deliver and store it. Do not send passwords, private videos or sensitive stream URLs.

We may disclose support correspondence when required by applicable law. The app does not automatically email us diagnostics or media.

04. Storage, security and retention

App preferences and private files use Android’s app storage protections. VIO disables Android app backup for its private data. These protections are not a promise of absolute security or a substitute for a secure device. VIO’s recycle bin is not an encrypted vault.

Local information remains until you clear it, reset app storage, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes temporary data. Original media and exported or shared copies can remain outside app storage. Android-managed trash follows the system’s expiry rules; the older-device VIO recycle bin has no automatic expiry implemented.

HTTPS streams encrypt data in transit when supported by the source. User-entered HTTP streams are also supported and are not encrypted in transit. Avoid HTTP links containing private information or access tokens.

We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request its deletion. This does not remotely erase media or app data on your phone.

05. Your controls and rights

You can revoke media access in Android Settings, turn off playback and queue history in VIO, clear saved playback history, or reset the app’s local storage. See Delete Data for exact steps and what each action removes.

There is no VIO account to delete. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold, object to or restrict certain processing, or complain to your local data protection authority. Contact vioplayer.support@gmail.com to make a request. We may need proportionate information to verify a request concerning support correspondence; never send an identity document unless specifically necessary and arranged securely.

06. Children and family use

Local playback does not require a name, date of birth or account. Parents and guardians can use the same device permissions and deletion controls. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate. Media content is selected by the device user; VIO does not provide a curated children’s media service.

07. This website and third parties

These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or forms that upload your data. They do not set their own cookies or browser-storage identifiers. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Following external links is subject to the destination’s policies.

08. Updates and contact

We will revise this page when our data practices change and update the date above. Material changes will be brought to users’ attention through the app or release information as appropriate; any required permission or consent will be obtained before the relevant processing. Planned advertising and subscriptions are not active in the version described here.
