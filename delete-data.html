Delete Data

You stay in control.

Clear VIO’s saved information, manage your media or request deletion of support correspondence.

No account. No cloud library. VIO player currently has no user accounts and no developer-hosted copy of your media or playback history. This page provides instructions; it cannot remotely erase your phone.

01. Clear watched positions and queues

  1. Open VIO player → Settings → History & privacy.
  2. Choose Clear playback history.
  3. Read the confirmation and tap Clear history.

This clears saved watched positions, last-played highlights and saved audio/video queues. It does not delete your media, favourites, playlists, bookmarks or general preferences. Stop playback first; playback can save a new position again.

To stop future history being saved, turn off Playback history, Remember video queue and Remember audio queue in the same category. Turning a switch off is not a substitute for clearing existing history.

02. Reset all local app data

Restore anything you need first. On Android 8–10, VIO’s recycle bin is inside app storage. Clearing app storage or uninstalling VIO permanently removes those recycled copies. Restore them before resetting the app.
  1. Stop playback and close VIO.
  2. Open your phone’s Settings → Apps → VIO player → Storage (sometimes “Storage & cache”).
  3. Select Clear storage or Clear data and confirm.

This removes private app preferences, library cache, history, queues, favourites, playlists, bookmarks, remembered selections and other private app files. Android menu names vary by device. Clear cache alone does not reset all saved app information.

Uninstalling VIO normally also removes its private app data. Your original media and exports in shared storage are not removed by an app reset or uninstall. Android-managed recycled files and copies held by other apps or cloud providers must be managed separately.

03. Delete actual video or music files

In VIO, open a media item’s menu, choose Delete, and select the recycle-bin or permanent-deletion option. Confirm Android’s approval prompt when shown. For several files, use selection mode.

To remove recycled files, open ⋮ → Recycle bin, select the items and use the permanent-delete action. Review the affected files before confirming. This can remove the actual files from your device, not just their entries in VIO.

On Android 11 and newer, Android manages trash and its expiry. On Android 8–10, VIO keeps recycled copies in private storage until you restore, permanently delete or reset/uninstall the app. Files deleted permanently may not be recoverable. Separate backups and previously shared copies are unaffected.

04. Revoke access to your library

Open Android Settings → Apps → VIO player → Permissions and change or remove video/music access. This stops future access covered by those permissions; it does not erase existing app preferences or your media. Reset app storage if you also want saved local app data removed.

05. Request deletion of support messages

If you previously contacted us, email vioplayer.support@gmail.com with the subject VIO player — data deletion request. Prefer the address used for the original conversation and identify which correspondence you want removed. Do not send your media or identity documents.

We will review the request and remove personal information we hold when applicable, except information that must be retained for legal obligations or unresolved disputes. We will explain any relevant exception. Copies retained independently by your email provider, GitHub or a streaming service are governed by those services. The current app has no separate account-deletion process because it has no accounts.
